# jupyterlab-plotly

## Summary
The JupyterLab extension for Plotly (installed in the Simplex viz_env). Enables Plotly's interactive charts (scatter plots, heatmaps, 3D plots) to render directly inside JupyterLab notebooks rather than as static images, enabling real-time data exploration during research analysis.
