# pydeck

## Summary
A Python wrapper around Uber's deck.gl WebGL visualization framework. Enables rendering of large-scale 3D geospatial visualizations (scatter layers, hexbin layers, arc layers) directly in Jupyter notebooks. Installed as a Jupyter nbextension in the Simplex research papers viz_env.
