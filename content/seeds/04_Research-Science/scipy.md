# scipy

## Summary
Python's primary scientific computing library, built on top of numpy. Provides critical tools for research: scipy.stats for hypothesis testing and distributions, scipy.optimize for curve fitting (e.g., Beer-Lambert fitting), scipy.signal for spectral analysis, and scipy.integrate for numerical integration.
